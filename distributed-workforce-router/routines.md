# Routines

## Morning triage

- **Schedule:** Weekdays at 09:00 in the user's local time.
- **Prompt:** List open commitments you were given. For each one, say whether you will do it, which catalog template fits, or that you are blocked on approval. Do not send, publish, purchase, or contact anyone. End with a numbered list of decisions that need a yes or no today.

## Catalog gap check

- **Schedule:** Mondays at 09:15 in the user's local time.
- **Prompt:** Read `catalog.json`. From the tasks discussed in the last week, name any recurring job that no entry covers. Propose at most one new bot title, with the folder name and a one-line summary. Do not create the bot. Wait for approval.
