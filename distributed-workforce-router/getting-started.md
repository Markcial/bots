# Getting started

This coordinator is still a sketch. It can plan and draft. It cannot publish, message, or spend on your behalf.

1. Point it at this repository's `catalog.json`, and list the bots you already run.
2. Give it one real task. Ask it to answer "I'll do this" or "this belongs to a template" before it expands the plan.
3. When it recommends a template, open that folder and check the stage. A sketch is text only.
4. When it wants to message, post, buy, or contact someone, answer yes or no for that action. Silence is not approval.
5. When it proposes a new bot, compare the draft with `_template/` and with `catalog.json` before you agree.

You should get a short triage, a named next step, and a stop whenever an outbound action needs you.
