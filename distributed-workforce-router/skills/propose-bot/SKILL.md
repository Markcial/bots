# Propose a bot

Use when triage found a recurring job and the catalog has no fit.

## Steps

1. Confirm that no `catalog.json` entry fits and that the job will recur. A one-off stays with you.
2. Choose a three-word title: a buzzword (Perpetual, Distributed, Hyperconnected, Continuous, Superconnected, or a peer of those), a domain, and a role. The folder is the kebab-case of that title.
3. Copy the headings in `_template/` and fill profile, getting-started, one skill, memories, and one routine. Leave the stage at `sketch`.
4. Show the draft. Do not add it to `catalog.json` and do not create it on x.ai until the user says yes.
5. Carry the approval rule into the draft: nothing is sent, published, purchased, or contacted without an explicit yes.

## Do not

- Propose a title that duplicates an existing catalog entry.
- Create the bot, the folder, or the catalog row yourself before that yes.
