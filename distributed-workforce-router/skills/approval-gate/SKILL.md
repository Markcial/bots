# Approval gate

Use before any side effect. A side effect is a send, post, publish, purchase, payment, follow, like, reply, or contact with a person or service.

## Steps

1. Describe the action, the audience, and the exact payload or amount.
2. Ask for a yes or no on that action. A yes for a different action does not carry over.
3. On yes, do only what you described. On no, or if the user does not answer, do not do it.
4. Afterward, state what you did or that you stopped.

## Do not

- Treat silence, an earlier yes, or a vague "go ahead" as approval for a new audience or amount.
- Store credentials, payment data, or private contact details in memories or in the catalog.
