# Media brief: Methodical Visual Synthesizer

Status: **media made, avatars approved**. The avatar set, the 16:9 and 1:1 motion videos, and the sample frame are in this folder. The owner approved the avatars on 2026-10-28. Nothing is published. This brief stays as the record of what was asked for.

## Title

Methodical Visual Synthesizer (chosen by the owner on 2026-10-28). File prefix: `mvs-`.

## Family look

Match the two finished avatars (Distributed Workforce Router: cyan glass beam; Perpetual Learning Machine: amber glass ring) and the Superconnected Research Entity brief (violet lattice). Each one is a single glass object with an inner glow, on a dark charcoal rounded square, lit from within, with soft bloom and a faint reflection below. One object, one colour, no text, no face, no mascot.

## Avatar

- **Colour:** rose. Glow `#FF5FA8`, deep tone `#C2185B`, highlight `#FFE0EE`. Background charcoal `#14161B` to `#1E2128`, the same rounded-square plate as the other avatars.
- **Motif (revised by the owner on 2026-10-28: "drawing, not arithmetic"):** a futuristic glass drawing canvas, a thin floating pane tilted in three-quarter view, with a sleek glass stylus or pencil whose tip is drawing one rose light stroke across it. The stroke is a single loose, hand-drawn gesture line (not a geometric arc) that is still being drawn, glowing brightest at the pencil tip. It says "it draws with you".
- **Concept in one line:** a floating glass canvas and a futuristic glass pencil drawing one glowing rose stroke, on charcoal.
- **Composition:** object centred, filling about 60% of the plate, slight three-quarter view so the glass thickness reads. The pencil tip and the fresh stroke are the brightest elements. A soft rose reflection sits under the canvas, like the Router's reflection.
- **Legibility:** at 48 px and 32 px it must still read as a pale pane with a bright rose stroke and a pencil. If the pencil detail turns to noise, simplify it for the small sizes.
- **Avoid:** rulers, set squares, compasses or anything that reads as measuring or arithmetic; a classic wooden pencil, a paintbrush, a palette or a flat pen-tool icon; anything ring-shaped (it would clash with the Learning Machine); any text or letters; a second colour.
- **Sizes to export:** 1024, 512, 256, 128, 48, 32 (`media/avatar-1024.png` and siblings), plus `media/sample-frame.png` from the video.

## Motion presentation

Same format as the Router and Learning Machine videos: 1920×1080, 30 fps, h264 with AAC audio, 16:9, plus a 1:1 cut. The finished videos run 30 seconds. Target here: **38 seconds**. Look: near-black background, a small numbered chip top left (for example `03 · EXPLICIT YES`), the avatar on the left, a headline on the right in rose and white, and a caption pill at the bottom that repeats the narration so the video works without sound. Built in code with Remotion, with the timings in data so lines can move without a re-edit.

- **Music:** a soft procedural lofi or ambient bed, ducked under the voice, about -14 LUFS overall, made in code in the same way as the existing videos (see [`LICENSE-music.md`](../../LICENSE-music.md)). No sampled or third-party material.
- **Deliverables:** `media/mvs-16x9.mp4`, `media/mvs-1x1.mp4`, `media/sample-frame.png`, and the avatar set above. The `mvs` prefix follows the initials of the title and changes if the title changes.

## Scope (owner, 2026-10-28)

The bot helps the owner create or track their brand (identity, palette, type, voice, a living brand book it keeps up to date and checks new work against) and makes any design work on top of it: images, avatars, social visuals, motion videos, explainers. The motion outline should show that range, not only avatars.
