# Media brief: Superconnected Research Entity

Status: **media made, avatars approved**. The avatar set, the 16:9 and 1:1 motion videos, and the sample frame are in this folder. The owner approved the avatars on 2026-10-28. Nothing is published. This brief stays as the record of what was asked for.

## Family look

Match the two finished avatars (Distributed Workforce Router: cyan glass beam; Perpetual Learning Machine: amber glass ring). Each one is a single glass object with an inner glow, on a dark charcoal rounded square, lit from within, with soft bloom and a faint reflection below. One object, one colour, no text, no face, no mascot.

## Avatar

- **Colour:** violet. Glow `#9B8CFF`, deep tone `#5B4BD6`, highlight `#E6E1FF`. Background charcoal `#14161B` to `#1E2128`, the same rounded-square plate as the other two.
- **Motif:** a lattice of connected glass beads (nodes). About eight to ten clear glass spheres joined by thin lines of violet light, arranged as a loose three-dimensional graph. One node near the centre is brighter than the rest, and a thin glass ring (a lens or probe ring) rests around it. The ring says "examined"; the lattice says "superconnected".
- **Concept in one line:** a web of glass nodes with one lit node under a thin lens ring, glowing violet on charcoal.
- **Composition:** object centred, filling about 60% of the plate, slight three-quarter view so the depth reads. Light filaments leave the lattice at two or three points, which hints at sources without drawing icons.
- **Legibility:** at 48 px and 32 px it must still read as a bright violet dot with a ring and a few spokes. If the lattice turns to noise, cut it down to five nodes for the small sizes.
- **Avoid:** magnifying-glass icon with a handle, a brain, a robot head, circuit-board clichés, any text or letters, a second colour.
- **Sizes to export:** 1024, 512, 256, 128, 48, 32 (`media/avatar-1024.png` and siblings), plus `media/sample-frame.png` from the video.

## Motion presentation

Same format as the Router and Learning Machine videos: 1920×1080, 30 fps, h264 with AAC audio, 16:9, plus a 1:1 cut. The finished videos run 30 seconds. Target here: **36 seconds**. Look: near-black background, a small numbered chip top left (for example `03 · EXPLICIT YES`), the avatar on the left, a headline on the right in violet and white, and a caption pill at the bottom that repeats the narration so the video works without sound. Built in code with Remotion, with the timings in data so lines can move without a re-edit.
