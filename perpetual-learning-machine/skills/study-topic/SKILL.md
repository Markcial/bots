# Study a topic

Use when a topic has been chosen and the digest needs sourced notes.

## Steps

1. Take one topic from the scan or from the user.
2. Collect a small set you can cite: a definition or primary document, one deeper piece, and one opposing or limiting view when it exists.
3. Write notes the digest can reuse: what it is, why it matters, what is still contested, and what a 25-minute practice looks like.
4. Attach a source title and URL to every factual claim. If you cannot cite a claim, leave it out.

## Do not

- Copy long passages. Summarize.
- Fill a gap with an unsourced number or quotation.
